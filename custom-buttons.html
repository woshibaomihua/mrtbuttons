What to include in your RFQ

The more of these you send, the faster and tighter the quote:

  • Size — in mm or ligne (L). Not sure? See our ligne size chart.
  • Material — resin, metal, corozo/horn, or "recommend for me" with the garment type.
  • Logo file — AI/EPS/PDF vector preferred; a clear photo works for quoting.
  • Quantity — per color/size, plus expected annual volume for better tiered pricing.
  • Color target — Pantone code or a fabric swatch you can mail/photograph.
  • Compliance needs — destination market (EU/US/JP) and any testing standards.

Mold & tooling policy

Embossing molds cost US$40–80 and remain your exclusive property — we never reuse a customer's mold for another buyer. The fee is credited back once cumulative orders pass 10,000 pieces. Molds are stored free for at least three years for instant reorders.

Before you send the RFQ

Custom button questions we answer daily

What is the minimum order for a custom button?

1,000 pieces per colour and size for custom production. The count is per colour and per size, so three colourways of one style is three minimums — consolidating colours is the fastest way to lower your commitment. Stock styles start from 100 pieces.

How much is the mold, and do I get it back?

A 3D embossed or debossed logo mold is a one-time US$40–80 depending on complexity, and we refund it against volume. Laser engraving needs no mold at all, which is why it is usually the right call for a first run or a short capsule.

Can you match our Pantone exactly?

Yes — we dye to match any Pantone or TPX reference, or a physical fabric swatch you post to us. Lab dips come back in 3–5 days and bulk dyeing only starts after your written approval, so the colour is signed off before any volume is committed.

What sizes can you make?

Standard rounds run 14L to 54L. Beyond that we make squares, flowers, toggles and shank styles, and fully bespoke 3D shapes from your CAD file or a physical sample.

How long does a custom order take end to end?

Roughly four weeks: RFQ reviewed within a day, digital proof by day three, mold and physical sample by day ten, bulk production days ten to twenty-five, then delivery. Samples in 5–7 days and bulk in 10–15 days once the sample is approved.

What compliance documents can you supply?

Tell us the material, garment application, destination market and retailer standard. We confirm the required material declarations, plating restrictions, needle-detection scope and third-party testing options before production.
