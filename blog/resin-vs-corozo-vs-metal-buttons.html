Resin vs Corozo vs Metal Buttons for Apparel Production

Choose resin for repeatable color and efficient volume programs, corozo for plant-based natural grain, and metal when weight, hardware function or deep logo relief matters. Final approval still depends on the garment, care process, attachment method, destination requirements and a physical production sample.

When resin wins

Resin is a practical starting point for shirts, polos, uniforms, childrenswear and private-label basics where repeatable color, face profile and production consistency matter. Specify the grade against the real wash and drying protocol, then approve color and dimensions on a physical sample. See our resin button line.

When corozo wins

Corozo suits premium shirting, tailoring and collections that want visible natural grain. It is made from tagua seed and can be dyed, but color and figure vary within and between lots. Environmental language should be limited to evidence held for the actual order rather than inferred from the material name alone. See corozo and horn options.

When metal wins

Metal is useful when the closure must deliver weight, hardware function, plating color or three-dimensional branding on blazers, coats, uniforms or denim. Approve the base metal, finish, attachment system and garment test together; the cap or face alone is not the full specification. See metal buttons and denim hardware.

A practical mixed-spec example

A mixed specification might use small resin buttons for a repeat-color shirt, corozo for a naturally varied tailoring program, an embossed metal button for a blazer and a matched tack-and-rivet system for denim.

The practical answer is rarely one material for an entire brand. Specify each garment first, compare like-for-like samples and consolidate only after the color, attachment and testing requirements are clear.

Frequently asked questions

Which button material is usually the lowest-cost option?

Standard resin buttons are generally the lower-cost starting point, but a usable comparison must hold size, construction, finish, logo work, color count, testing and quantity constant. Ask for like-for-like quotations rather than relying on a public unit price.

Which button material is best for uniforms or frequent washing?

Choose only after sharing the full care protocol, including wash temperature, chemistry, drying method and number of cycles. A suitable resin grade or metal construction can then be sampled and tested on the finished garment; no material should be approved from a generic wash claim alone.

Is corozo automatically more sustainable than resin?

Corozo is a plant-derived material with natural grain, while resin is manufactured for consistency and can have different feedstocks. A sustainability claim still needs evidence for the actual material, processing, traceability and certification scope used in the order.

Which material is best for a custom logo button?

Metal supports deep relief, embossing and enamel-style details; resin supports engraving, printing and molded profiles; corozo supports engraving while retaining natural grain. Logo size, line width, finish and garment use should be checked on a physical sample.

What should a buyer send for a material comparison quote?

Send the button diameter or ligne size, construction, garment and fabric, color reference, finish, logo artwork, quantity by color and size, care process, destination market and required testing. These inputs allow the three materials to be compared on the same specification.
