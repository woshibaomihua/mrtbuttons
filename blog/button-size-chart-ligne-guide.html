Button Size Chart: Ligne (L) to MM Conversion

Quick conversion: 1 ligne (1L) = 0.635 mm. A 10 mm button is approximately 16L, a 15 mm button is approximately 24L, a 24L button is 15.24 mm, and a 28L button is 17.78 mm (normally listed as 18 mm).

Button sizes are quoted in ligne (written “L”), a unit still used on garment tech packs. Convert ligne to millimeters by multiplying by 0.635; convert millimeters to ligne by dividing by 0.635.

Working backwards: millimetres to ligne

The formula: ligne = millimetres ÷ 0.635, and millimetres = ligne × 0.635. So a 28L button is 18 mm, and a 20 mm button is 32L. If you have measured a button off a sample garment with a ruler, this is the direction you need.

Measure the diameter across the face of the button, not including the shank. Anything above roughly 54L (34 mm) is decorative or craft territory rather than a standard garment size — a 58 mm disc is about 91L, which we would quote as a made-to-order piece rather than pull from a standard range.

Three rules of thumb when choosing size

1. Match the buttonhole, not the other way round

A buttonhole should be the button diameter plus 2–3 mm (plus thickness for domed or shank buttons). If the garment is already patterned, measure the buttonhole and work backwards.

2. Heavier fabric, bigger button

Fabric weight and button size scale together: 18L on poplin shirting, 32L on a wool blazer, 44L on a Melton overcoat. An undersized button on heavy fabric slips; an oversized one distorts light fabric.

3. Keep thickness in mind for machine sewing

Automatic button-sew machines have thickness limits (commonly 2–4 mm for flat 4-hole buttons). If your factory sews by machine, confirm button thickness as well as diameter — we list both on every quotation.

Jeans buttons and snaps use millimeters, not ligne

Denim tack buttons are sold by mm: 17 mm for most fashion jeans, 20 mm for heavyweight, 22–25 mm for workwear. Snap fasteners are likewise quoted by cap diameter (9.5–20 mm). When in doubt, mail us a sample garment and we'll measure and match it.

Button size conversion questions

How many millimeters is 1 ligne?

1 ligne equals 0.635 millimeters. Multiply the ligne size by 0.635 to convert it to mm.

What button size is 10 mm in ligne?

10 mm is 15.75 ligne, so the nearest standard button size is 16L.

What button size is 15 mm in ligne?

15 mm is 23.62 ligne, so the nearest standard button size is 24L.

What is a 24L button size in millimeters?

24L equals 15.24 mm and is commonly rounded to 15 mm in button size charts.

What is a 28L button size in millimeters?

28L equals 17.78 mm and is commonly rounded to 18 mm.
