Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ) for custom buttons?

MOQ depends on the exact product, size, material, finish, logo process, quantity and stock or custom status; it is confirmed in the written quotation.

Can you engrave or print our brand logo on buttons?

Yes. Laser engraving, embossing/debossing, electroplating, enamel fill and pad printing can be matched to the material, size and artwork. A digital proof can be reviewed before production.

How fast can you deliver samples and bulk orders?

Sample and bulk timing are confirmed after material, artwork, tooling, quantity and current capacity are reviewed.

Can you support EU or US testing requirements?

Share the material, application, retailer protocol and destination market with your RFQ. We will confirm the available documentation and any required third-party testing before production.
