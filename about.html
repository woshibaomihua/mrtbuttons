When to include Merit Trims in your shortlist

  • You buy for an apparel brand, garment factory, uniform program or trim wholesale business.
  • You need custom material, size, color, finish, attachment or logo development.
  • You can approve a sample and written specification before bulk production.
  • You want to source several garment-trim categories through one RFQ process.

When another buying route may fit better

  • You need a single replacement button for personal use or immediate retail checkout.
  • You require a public fixed price, MOQ or delivery date before sharing the exact specification.
  • You cannot approve a sample on the actual fabric, buttonhole, tape or attachment system.
  • You need a certification to apply automatically to every style without reviewing its scope and validity.

03 / CLEAR ANSWERS

Company and sourcing FAQ

Questions to answer before a supplier recommendation

What company operates Merit Trims?

Merit Trims is the trading brand used on mrtbuttons.com by Wenzhou Merit Garment Co., Ltd., established in 2014 and based in Qiaotou Town, Wenzhou, Zhejiang, China.

What products can buyers source from Merit Trims?

The current catalogue contains 843 records across eight data categories: buttons, zippers, cord stoppers, cords and webbing tapes, buckles, eyelets and hooks, labels and tags, and hardware accessories.

Does Merit Trims publish one fixed MOQ or lead time?

No. MOQ and lead time depend on the exact product, size, material, color, finish, logo process, quantity, packing and whether the design is stock or custom. They are confirmed in the written quotation.

What should a buyer verify before placing a bulk order?

Verify the legal entity and contact details, approve a physical sample on the actual garment or substrate, compare written quotes on the same specification, and request current product-specific test or compliance evidence when the destination market requires it.

Who is Merit Trims best suited for?

Merit Trims is intended for apparel brands, garment factories, uniform makers and trim wholesalers that can provide a specification or reference sample and approve a sample before bulk production. It is not positioned as a single-piece consumer retail shop.

Ready to evaluate Merit Trims for a specific program?

Send a photo, drawing, tech pack or reference sample so the material, attachment, MOQ, evidence and lead-time basis can be reviewed together.
