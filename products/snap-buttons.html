We supply the three workhorse families of snap fasteners: spring (S-spring) snaps for shirts and light jackets, ring snaps for heavier outerwear and bags, and prong snaps that distribute attachment around knit and jersey fabrics. Four-part sets combine the cap, socket, stud and post; brass and stainless options offer better corrosion resistance than iron-core constructions, but the selected finish and attachment still need validation through the buyer's wash process.

Caps can be engraved, embossed, printed or finished with a colored lacquer or pearl insert to match your design. For babywear, put the required EN 71, CPSIA or retailer-specific test scope in the tech pack and agree the snap-force and pull-off test plan before sampling.

Approve the cap, socket, stud, post and matched die as one setting system. Fabric stack thickness, reinforcement and press travel determine whether the snap clinches correctly and stays aligned in production. See the snap failure guide for the pre-production checks.

Frequently asked questions

What are prong snaps?

Prong snaps use a ring of metal prongs that pierce the fabric and clinch into the matching socket or stud. The distributed attachment is well suited to babywear, shirts and other lightweight or knitted garments.

What's the difference between spring snaps and ring snaps?

Spring snaps use two parallel S-springs in the socket — lighter action, ideal for shirts. Ring snaps use a circular wire ring — stronger retention for jackets, bags and workwear.

Do you supply attaching dies and presses?

Matched die sets are available for the selected snap size and should be confirmed against your hand press, pneumatic press or setting machine before production.

Will brass snaps rust after laundering?

Solid brass and stainless components resist rust better than iron-core snaps. Confirm the core material, finish and wash test for the finished garment before bulk production.

Can snap caps carry our logo?

Yes. Depending on cap size and construction, logos can be engraved, embossed, printed or combined with a colored lacquer or decorative insert.

What should I send for a snap button quote?

Send the snap family and size, cap design, fabric composition and total stack thickness, finish, quantity, wash process and available setting machine or die reference.
