Custom metal clothing labels—also searched as metal garment tags, logo plates and nameplates—are permanent brand trims for apparel, bags, footwear and accessories. Quick answer: a metal clothing label is normally fixed to the finished product; a hang tag is normally removable retail or product-information packaging. Buyers may also call the permanent part a metal logo tag, brand plate, nameplate or metal hem tag. Name the placement and substrate in the RFQ so the attachment is specified correctly.

Send these details for a comparable quote

  • Artwork: AI, EPS, PDF or SVG vector file, plus the minimum readable logo detail.
  • Dimensions: finished width, height and thickness in millimetres.
  • Application: product type, exact placement and a photo or tech-pack drawing of the substrate.
  • Construction: preferred attachment, finish or plating reference, color fill and edge profile.
  • Order plan: quantity per design, size and finish; packaging; destination; and any required wash, rub, pull or corrosion standard.

Frequently asked questions

Can you make our own logo as a metal clothing label?

Yes. Send the logo artwork, finished dimensions, target product, preferred finish, attachment method and quantity. Feasibility, tooling, MOQ, sample schedule and pricing are then confirmed for that exact construction.

What finishes are available for metal garment tags?

The examples on this page include shiny or matte gold, silver, gunmetal, black, rose-gold and antique-style tones. The achievable tone and texture depend on the base construction and approved physical sample.

How are metal labels attached to clothing or bags?

Possible structures include sewing holes, side tabs, fold-in legs, loops, hanging rings and rivet-ready holes. Choose the method against the actual fabric, leather or panel stack and validate it on production substrate before bulk.

What is the difference between a metal clothing label and a hang tag?

A metal clothing label or logo plate is normally a permanent brand trim attached to the product. A hang tag is normally removable retail or product-information packaging. State which function you need in the RFQ.

What should a metal label RFQ include?

Include vector artwork, outer dimensions, target product and placement, attachment method, finish reference, color-fill requirements, quantity per design or finish, packaging needs and any required wash, rub or corrosion test.

What should be checked on a metal label sample?

Check dimensions, logo detail, edge profile, finish and color, attachment fit and placement on the real substrate. Add use-specific wash, rub, pull or corrosion checks when the finished product requires them.
